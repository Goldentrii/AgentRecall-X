## Gate design

- **Trigger**: `finalResults[0]?.calibrated < CONFIDENCE_FLOOR.medium` (0 when
  no results at all). Placed *after* the existing Bridge block so the
  Bridge's own low-confidence filter never tries to re-drill an archive item
  into itself.
- **Score containment**: every archive item's score is
  `Math.min(keywordExactness(query, line), CONFIDENCE_FLOOR.medium - 0.01)` on
  the `"cosine"` scale — structurally incapable of landing "medium"/"high".
- **Label + provenance**: excerpt is prefixed
  `[raw-archive · low-confidence · journal/archive/raw/<file>]`.
- **Cap**: `ARCHIVE_SOURCE_CAP = 3` items per call, unranked beyond
  newest-file-first / top-to-bottom-within-file (same line-grep flavor as
  `journalSearch`, not a competing ranked source).
- **Excluded** from `total_searched`/`candidates_by_source` (those describe
  the 3-source RRF fan-out only); included in `sources_queried` whenever the
  gate runs, regardless of hit count (matches the existing "queried ≠ had
  hits" convention already used for palace/journal/insight).
